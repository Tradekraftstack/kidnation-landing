# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Parents exploring an enriching entertainment world for children aged 5–8, often on a phone alongside their child.

## Product Purpose
Introduce KidNation through delight, then help families explore its curated videos and music, Word Ninja, and guided Storyteller. The app is freemium and has zero ads. Unreleased games and unverified metrics must not appear as live claims.

## Brand Personality
Playful, confident, warm. Premium children's animation with six canon friends: Salome, Nari, Melli, Jordan, Bjorn, and Arjun. Preserve existing character artwork and brand typography.

## Anti-references
No generic SaaS cards, clinical education branding, babyish visuals, invented character designs, or chaotic motion.

## Design Principles
Lead with imaginative play. Keep parent-facing information clear. Preserve existing page sections below the new opening. Use existing assets rather than inventing character likenesses.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic navigation, visible keyboard focus, readable contrast, responsive layout, motion controls, and prefers-reduced-motion support.

## Approved Task Direction
Damon approved the floating-world opening on November 24, 2026: purple imagination world, oversized KidNation lettering, floating books, headphones, stars, existing characters, pointer depth, lighter mobile motion, and the original page below.
